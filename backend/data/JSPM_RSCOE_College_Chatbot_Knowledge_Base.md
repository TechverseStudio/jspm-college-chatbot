# JSPM Tathawade Campus --- College Chatbot Knowledge Base

**Prepared:** 18 October 2026\
**Purpose:** Starter knowledge source for an AI chatbot being developed
in Antigravity.\
**Research scope:** Official institute websites supplied by the project
owner, plus official pages linked from those websites and official pages
indexed by search.

## IMPORTANT INSTRUCTIONS FOR THE CHATBOT

1.  Treat this document as a **starter knowledge base**, not a complete
    or permanently current source.
2.  Answer only from information in this document or from approved,
    current official documents added later.
3.  Never guess fees, cutoffs, intake, package statistics, bus routes,
    hostel charges, office hours, or officials' contact details.
4.  When information is missing, old, contradictory, or unclear, say so
    plainly and direct the user to the relevant official website/contact
    listed here.
5.  For admissions, fees, scholarships, cutoffs, seat availability, and
    eligibility, explain that rules can change each academic year and
    should be checked against the current Maharashtra State CET Cell /
    DTE / FRA notice and the institute's latest notice.
6.  Keep information institute-specific. Do not mix RSCOE Engineering
    information with Pharmacy, Education, Management Studies, or
    Polytechnic unless a shared campus-level source explicitly says it
    applies to all.
7.  Do not describe an old intake or eligibility rule as current. The
    pages below contain some legacy pages; the academic year is recorded
    where known.
8.  Cite or link the source page in answers whenever possible.
9.  Personal mobile numbers are included only when published as official
    admission/department contacts on the institute websites. Do not
    infer private contact information.
10. This file was assembled from publicly accessible website content and
    search-indexed official pages on 18 October 2026. Some supplied
    sites returned access errors to the research tool; those areas are
    marked as requiring verification.

### 2.7 Scholarship and fee concession guidance

    waiver based only on category.

    rules, and the institute scholarship section.

    range, and admission route applies, but avoid collecting sensitive

## 8. Recommended chatbot response pattern

When the source contains the answer: - Give the direct answer first. -
State the academic year or date associated with it. - Include the
official source link. - Add a brief warning if the information is
subject to annual revision.

When the source does not contain the answer: \> "I couldn't verify that
detail from the currently available official information. Please check
\[relevant official page\] or contact \[published office/contact\].
Fees, cutoffs, intake and admission rules can change each academic
year."

When official pages conflict: - Explain that the official pages show
different figures. - Do not select one arbitrarily. - Link both sources
and direct the user to the responsible office for confirmation.
